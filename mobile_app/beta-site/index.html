This is a no-money design beta

Balances and contributions are simulated and have no cash value. Demo care providers are fictional. The app cannot collect money, book care, approve treatment, provide insurance or pay a clinic or pharmacy.

Your testing mission

Use it like it’s yours.

Try the everyday journeys, notice what feels unclear, and tell us where the experience gets in your way.

01

Join and return

Create an account, verify your email, set your six-digit app PIN, close the app and unlock it again.

02

Shape your profile

Edit personal and emergency-contact details, restart the app and confirm your saved information returns.

03

Explore saving

Create or edit a saving instruction and add a simulated contribution. No money will move.

04

Try Family Pocket

Create a shared pocket, invite another verified tester and try accepting, declining or removing access.

05

Find demo care

Search and filter the fictional directory, view details and try the demo QR or manual-code journey.

06

Send honest feedback

Report what you tried, what happened and what you expected. Small observations are useful too.

Install on Android

Three quick steps.

  1. 1

    Download the APK

    Open this private page on your Android phone and tap the download button.

  2. 2

    Allow this installation

    If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps. This permission applies only to that source.

  3. 3

    Install and open

    Approve the installation, open HealthPocket Dev and begin the test. You can turn the install permission off afterwards.

Android may show a safety prompt because this private beta is not distributed through Google Play. Only install the file from the link shared in the official early-testers group.
