PART A — PRIVACY NOTICE (DRAFT)

Who we are
HealthPocket is operated by [OPERATOR NAME AND ADDRESS]. For questions about
your information, contact gethealthpocket@gmail.com.

What this beta does
This beta lets adults test account setup, savings plans, Family Pockets, and
provider discovery. Contributions and balances are simulated: they are not
money held for you. Demo provider listings are fictional, not confirmed partners.
Real account information is still personal information even in a no-money beta.

Information used in the app
- Account identifiers, email, sign-in provider, and verification status.
- Profile details you enter: name, optional phone number, birth date, gender,
  address, state, and emergency-contact name and phone number.
- Savings settings, simulated contributions, and notification preferences.
- Family Pocket membership, invitations, roles, and invitation responses.
- Information you choose to send for support or feedback.
- Camera access only when you open the demo QR scanner. This implementation
  reads a demo provider identifier; it does not upload camera images to our
  backend. You can deny camera access and paste a demo code instead.
- [CONFIRM AND DESCRIBE SERVICE LOGS, DEVICE/NETWORK DATA, AND ANY ANALYTICS.]

Why we use it
To sign you in, restore your account, show your settings and histories, manage
Family Pocket invitations and permissions, answer support requests, and keep
the service working safely. Notification preferences alone do not mean every
notification channel is already available. Directory filtering currently runs
on your device; this feature does not request GPS location.

What this beta is not collecting or doing
The beta does not need identity-document uploads, BVN/NIN verification, medical
records, or bank credentials. Do not send those in feedback. Simulated savings
do not trigger payments, investment purchases, insurance, or care approval.
Proposed commitment, subject to owner confirmation: we do not sell your
personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Sign-in and your app PIN
Firebase Authentication handles email/password or Google sign-in. The six-digit
HealthPocket PIN unlocks the app locally; it is not your Firebase password.
The app stores a salted PIN verifier using device secure storage, not a
plaintext PIN or a PIN in ordinary Firestore documents. Keep your PIN private.

Who can see information
Your private pocket is not public. Family Pocket members can see shared
membership and contribution information; invitation access is restricted by
role and the invited verified email. A shared pocket is not a private diary.
Google/Firebase provides authentication and database services. Support email
uses Gmail and your chosen email provider. People receiving your invitations
or shared-pocket information may see the information needed for that journey.

Other people's information
Ask your emergency contact before entering their details. Only invite people
you have a genuine reason to invite. The beta does not support child accounts

Feedback and WhatsApp
Opening the email support action creates a draft in your email app; HealthPocket

PART B — NO-MONEY BETA TERMS (DRAFT)

Purpose and eligibility
This is an early test of HealthPocket for adults aged 18 or older. Features can
be incomplete and may contain errors. Use your own account and accurate contact
details. Keep your sign-in credentials and PIN private.

No money or care entitlement
Do not transfer money based on a beta balance or contribution record. These
records have no cash value and do not represent a deposit, investment, insurance
policy, or promise to pay a clinic. Demo listings do not establish partnerships.
The directory does not book appointments or authorize treatment. The DEV QR
journey is local role-play: requests disappear when its screen is closed,
operator actions are simulated, and no real care or settlement is authorized.

Use the app responsibly
Do not access someone else's account, exploit the service, submit abusive
content, or invite people without a genuine reason. Share only information
you are entitled to share. The service is not medical advice or an emergency
response channel; do not delay seeking care because of anything in the beta.

Feedback and availability
You can send feedback to gethealthpocket@gmail.com or the private beta group.
Do not send secrets, bank credentials, or medical records. We may change beta
features as we learn. If test records need resetting, explain the impact first
and follow the published retention/privacy process rather than silently
discarding personal information.

Leaving the beta
You can stop using the app. Contact support about account/data deletion; the
published Privacy Notice must explain the working process before release.

Questions and future versions
Contact gethealthpocket@gmail.com. Real financial or partner-care services, if
introduced later, need separate arrangements and updated information before
users can use them. Nothing here removes rights that apply to users.
